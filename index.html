Who we are

Working Towards Better Health Around the World

The World Health Organization (WHO) is a specialized agency of the United Nations responsible for international public health.

It works with countries and partners to promote health, protect people from health emergencies and improve access to healthcare.

Our purpose

Health Is a Foundation for Development
