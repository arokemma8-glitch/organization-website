Who we are

Promoting Health for Everyone

The World Health Organization is a specialized agency of the United Nations established in 1948.

WHO works internationally to promote health, coordinate responses to health emergencies and support countries in strengthening their health systems.

Its work involves governments, health professionals, researchers and other partners working together to address global health challenges.
