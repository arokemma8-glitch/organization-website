Community participation

Be an Agent of Positive Change

Community participation is important in improving public health. Individuals can support healthy communities through responsible actions.

  • Practice good hygiene and sanitation.
  • Share accurate health information.
  • Support disease prevention activities.
  • Encourage people to seek professional healthcare.
  • Participate in local health awareness activities.
